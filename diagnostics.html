A coordinated service, not a drop-off

Diagnostics that connect to a decision

A test only has value when the result is reliable, reviewed and used. Our role is to make sure the right test is done, that the sample is handled properly, and that a clinician has actually looked at the result before it goes anywhere.

  • Right test, right reason. We confirm which investigation is actually needed for the requesting institution rather than running broad panels.
  • Correct identification and handling. Samples are collected, labelled, transported and processed with appropriate identification and chain of custody.
  • Clinician review. Results are interpreted by a qualified professional before release, with abnormal or unexpected findings explained.
  • Reports that travel well. Reports are provided in the format the destination institution accepts, including certified copies where needed.
  • Home collection. Mobile laboratory sample collection is available where clinically suitable and within our service coverage.

Not sure which test you need?

Send us the requirement from the requesting institution — a letter, an email or a form. We will confirm what is clinically indicated and what is required to satisfy the request. Tests are never run without a clinical indication.

The process

How testing is arranged

Requirement confirmed

We review the request, the clinical indication and what the receiving institution actually needs.

Tests ordered

A clinician determines which investigations are appropriate and arranges the collection.

Sample collected

At the clinic or, where suitable, at your home through mobile collection with proper identification and transport.

Results reviewed

A qualified professional reviews the results and discusses anything unexpected with you.

Report issued

Reports and certificates are prepared in the required format and released to you or the requesting institution.

Home collection

Mobile laboratory sample collection

For patients who find attending a clinic difficult, or who need ongoing monitoring, samples can be collected at home with appropriate identification, handling, transport and laboratory coordination.

  • Convenient collection at home, scheduled around your treatment
  • Correct patient identification and specimen labelling before dispatch
  • Transport under appropriate conditions to the laboratory
  • Results reviewed by a clinician and reported to you directly

Coverage and suitability apply.

Mobile collection is subject to clinical suitability, service availability and geographic coverage. Some investigations require a clinical setting, and we will tell you if that applies.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a doctor's order for diagnostic tests?

Yes. Tests are ordered by a qualified clinician with a clinical indication — which may be your own prescriber or the clinician who assesses you here. We will tell you whether we can arrange the order or whether you need a referral.

How long do results take?

It depends on the investigation. Routine profiles are typically available within one to three working days; specialised and confirmatory tests take longer. We give you a realistic estimate when the test is ordered, and will contact you if a result needs action.

Can I get my results at home?

Where mobile collection is available, yes. Otherwise samples are collected at the clinic and results are shared with you directly, with a clinician available to explain anything unexpected.

Will you share my results with an employer or institution?

Only with your explicit written request, and only the documents required for that purpose. Detailed clinical information is not shared unless it is necessary and you have authorised it.

Do you accept external laboratory results?

Yes, where the report is from an accredited laboratory, is complete and is clinically valid, and where the requesting institution accepts it. We will review the report before it is used to support a certificate or assessment.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How quickly will I hear back?

Appointment requests sent during clinic hours are usually answered within one working day. If your request is urgent — for example a possible rabies exposure, or a medication supply that will run out tomorrow — please call or message us directly rather than waiting for an email reply.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Your prescriptions (or clear photographs), all medicines in your possession, a list of your medical conditions and allergies, and any form or letter from the institution requesting a certificate or assessment. For travel-related appointments, bring your itinerary and departure date.

Do you charge for a consultation?

Consultation and service fees vary. We provide a clear written estimate before any non-emergency service is arranged, and we will tell you if any additional costs — such as laboratory tests, consumables or delivery — apply.

Can I be seen in a language other than English?

Our team works in English and Nepali. If you would prefer another language, let us know when you enquire and we will confirm what interpretation support can be arranged for your appointment.

Can my family member or employer request an appointment on my behalf?

Yes, with your consent. Because of patient confidentiality we will confirm details directly with you before the appointment. For employer or institutional requests, we will also need the specific requirement in writing.

What if I have a medical emergency?

Do not wait for an appointment. Contact the nearest emergency service or go to the nearest hospital immediately. Clinic Pharmacy does not provide emergency or ambulance services, and our home-care services are not a substitute for emergency care.

Your information

Privacy notice

How we handle the information you share with us.

Information you provide through this website or during an appointment is used only to respond to your enquiry, arrange and deliver the services you request, and maintain accurate clinical records. Clinical information is treated as confidential and is released to third parties — including employers, universities, airlines, insurers or immigration authorities — only where you have given explicit authorisation for that specific document, or where disclosure is required by law.

We do not sell personal or medical information. We retain consultation and clinical records for the period required by professional and legal standards, after which they are securely disposed of. You may request access to, or correction of, the information we hold about you.

Using this service

Terms of service

  • Information on this website is general information and does not constitute medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
  • An appointment request is not a confirmed booking until you receive confirmation from our team.
  • All prescriptions, treatment decisions, vaccination decisions and medical certificates are issued only by appropriately qualified and authorised healthcare professionals following an individual clinical assessment.
  • Vaccine availability, eligibility and administration schedules are confirmed before booking and may change with supply.
  • Controlled and psychotropic medicines are handled in accordance with applicable Nepalese law and the requirements of the destination country. No assurance is given that any medicine may cross any border.
  • Home-care services are subject to clinical suitability, staffing, service availability and geographic coverage, and may be declined where care cannot be delivered safely at home.
  • Estimates and quotations are valid for the period stated and may be revised if the clinical requirement changes.
We want to improve

Complaints & feedback

If something has not met your expectations, please tell us. Contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email with a description of what happened and, where relevant, the date and the service involved. We will acknowledge your message, review the record and respond with our findings and the action taken.

If you are not satisfied with the outcome of a review, you may ask for it to be reconsidered, and we will arrange for the matter to be reviewed by a senior clinician who was not directly involved.

Who this is for

Healthcare is confusing enough without a language barrier

Visitors to Nepal often face unfamiliar systems, paperwork in a second language, and uncertainty about how to access the right care. Our role is to make that part of the trip straightforward.

  • International visitors on short stays who need consultations, medication or a certificate.
  • Long-stay visitors and expatriates setting up ongoing medication supply and routine healthcare.
  • Patients travelling to Nepal for treatment who need documentation, diagnostics and coordinated appointments.
  • Relatives accompanying a patient who need practical guidance on what happens next.
  • Visitors needing home care in private accommodation where a clinic visit is impractical.

Before, during and after

Support across the whole stay

Most visitors need help at three distinct moments. We cover all three.

Before you arrive

  • Travel-health consultation and vaccination planning
  • Medication supply planning and prescription documentation
  • Guidance on medical paperwork needed for your journey
  • Arranging necessary approvals before arrival where possible

During your stay

  • Coordination of diagnostic testing and appropriate clinical appointments
  • Medical certificate coordination for eligible travellers
  • Home-care arrangements for visitors staying in private accommodation, where available
  • Medication supply and repeat prescriptions during a long stay

Before you leave

  • Guidance on accessing suitable follow-up care before departure
  • Consolidated medical records and reports to take home
  • Prescription and medication documentation for your return journey
  • Referral or handover to your home-country clinician where appropriate
Working with us

Practical things you can expect

Arranging healthcare in another country raises fair questions about cost, timing and trust. Here is how we work.

  • Written estimates first. Where a service has a predictable cost, we tell you before it happens â€” not afterwards.
  • Plain language. Clinical findings, test results and documents are explained in terms you can use, with professional interpretation available where needed.
  • Records you can take home. Summaries, reports and prescriptions are consolidated so your home-country clinician can pick up where we left off.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I get medical care in Nepal as a foreign visitor?

Yes. We coordinate consultations, diagnostics, medication supply, certificates and home care for international visitors. The cost of treatment for foreigners is not covered by the Government of Nepal, so we always provide a clear written estimate before any non-emergency service is arranged.

Do you speak languages other than English?

Our team works primarily in English and Nepali. For visitors who would prefer another language, we arrange interpretation support where the situation allows. Please mention your preference when you contact us and we will confirm what can be arranged.

Can you help if I need treatment for a condition I already have?

Yes â€” we coordinate appointments, diagnostics and medication continuity, and we can provide documentation for your insurer. The clinical decisions remain with the treating professional, and where a case is complex we will involve the appropriate specialist.

Will my travel insurance cover the services?

That depends on your policy. We can provide itemised invoices, clinical reports and letters that insurers commonly require, and we will make sure your documentation is complete â€” but only your insurer can confirm coverage.

What if I need medication refilled while I am in Nepal?

Send us your prescription and the supply you have left. We will review it, coordinate the supply where it is lawful to do so, and advise on what you need to carry when you return home.

How do I arrange follow-up care when I get home?

Before you leave we provide a consolidated summary â€” findings, treatments, prescriptions and outstanding actions â€” which you can share with your home-country clinician. Where continued specialist follow-up is needed, we can identify the appropriate pathway.

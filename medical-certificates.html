Medical fitness & diagnostic clearances

Clearances that are valid, current and actually accepted

Services

Four assessments, one consistent standard

Each assessment is scoped to the requesting institution's requirements — we confirm what is needed before we begin.

Fit-to-fly assessments

Clinical evaluation for individuals who need to establish whether they can travel by air, including assessment of relevant symptoms, recent illness, surgery, existing medical conditions and any airline-specific requirements. Additional airline medical forms may be needed.

Fit-to-work assessments

Medical evaluations for employment, workplace placement or occupational requirements. Assessments may include a medical history, physical examination and relevant investigations according to the employer's requirements and the individual's role.

Fit-to-travel assessments

Individualised assessment of health considerations associated with planned travel, including existing medical conditions, medication needs and destination-related risks.

Diagnostic evaluations & medical certificates

Coordination of clinically indicated laboratory tests, examinations and supporting reports for travel, employment, educational institutions, insurance or other legitimate administrative requirements.

How it works

From request to signed certificate

Certificates are rejected for predictable reasons — the wrong test, a missing signature, an expired validity period, or a form that does not match the institution's template. We remove those failure points early.

  1. We confirm the requirement. Which country, employer, university or airline, and what exact form or wording is needed?
  2. We scope the assessment. Examination, investigations, photographs or vaccination records required for that specific request.
  3. The clinical review. A qualified professional assesses you, reviews records and orders only what is clinically indicated.
  4. Testing & reports. Any required investigations are coordinated, with results reviewed before the certificate is prepared.
  5. Issue & handover. The certificate is issued, signed, dated and handed over with any supporting reports and a validity statement.

Before you arrive

What we need from you

Bring everything you have. Missing documents are the most common cause of a delayed certificate.

Documents

Documents

  • The exact form or template required, if you have been given one
  • Letter of request from the employer, university, embassy or airline
  • Existing medical records, reports and investigation results
  • Previous certificates, vaccination records and medication list
  • Passport or national identity document where identification is required
  • Recent photographs if the institution requires them

Health information

  • Current diagnoses and recent illness, surgery or hospital admission
  • All medicines and supplements you take, including doses
  • Allergies, particularly to medicines, latex and anaesthetics
  • Pregnancy status where relevant and clinically appropriate
  • Hearing, visual or mobility needs requiring accommodation
  • Family history relevant to the assessment

Certificates are issued only after the relevant assessment

The scope of investigations and documentation depends on the requesting institution's requirements. We will tell you, in advance, what is needed and what it will involve.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How long does a fit-to-fly assessment take?

Most assessments are completed in a single visit once any required investigations are available. Where blood tests, imaging or a specialist opinion are needed, the certificate is issued after those results are reviewed — usually within a few working days. We will tell you the realistic timeline when you book.

Do airline-specific medical forms need a particular doctor?

Some airlines and aviation authorities require a form completed or countersigned by a specific clinician or an aviation medical examiner. Tell us the airline and the exact form at the time of booking and we will confirm whether we can complete it.

What happens if I am not fit to fly or work?

We will explain the findings honestly, outline what the implications are for your proposed travel or role, and — where appropriate — discuss adjustments with your prescriber or employer. Our role is clinical clarity, not a guaranteed outcome.

Can a certificate be issued remotely?

Sometimes, where the institution accepts a telehealth assessment and you have recent examination records from the same clinician. Most certificates require an in-person examination, and we will confirm what is possible for your specific request.

Will my results be shared with anyone else?

Your medical information is handled confidentially. It is released only to you, or where you have asked us to send a certificate or report directly to an institution — and only the documents required for that purpose.
